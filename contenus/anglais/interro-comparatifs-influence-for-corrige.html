Corrigé · Interro blanche

Corrige-toi phrase par phrase avec le barème. Une seule faute dans la structure (than, to, participe) suffit à perdre le point.

Ex. 1 · 5 ptsEx. 2 · 5 ptsEx. 3 · 4 ptsEx. 4 · 6 pts

1Comparatifs · 0,5 pt par phrase

  1. My new phone is lighter than my old one.Adjectif court : -er + than.
  2. This exam was easier than the last one.Deux syllabes en -y : y → ier.
  3. In France, football is more popular than rugby.Adjectif long : more + adj + than.
  4. Her English is better than mine.Irrégulier : good → better.
  5. The weather today is worse than yesterday.bad → worse (pas worst, qui est le superlatif).
  6. My sister is less patient than me.Infériorité : less + adj + than. than I am est aussi juste.
  7. The film is as funny as the book.Égalité : as … as.
  8. Our flat isn't as big as (so big as) yours.Égalité au négatif : as … as ou so … as.
  9. The test was much more difficult than I had expected.much renforce le comparatif ; difficult est long.
  10. Today is hotter than yesterday.Consonne doublée : hot → hotter.

2Structures d'influence · 1 pt par phrase

  1. My mother made me tidy my room before going out.make + qqn + verbe sans to.
  2. I'm going to have my bike repaired this weekend.have + objet + participe passé : on fait réparer par quelqu'un d'autre.
  3. Our teacher doesn't let us use our phones in class.let + qqn + verbe sans to.
  4. I finally got my dad to buy me a new laptop.get + qqn + to + verbe (convaincre).
  5. We were made to stay after class.Le piège : au passif, make reprend to.

3for + quelqu'un + to · 1 pt par phrase

  1. The river is too cold for the children to swim in.La préposition in reste à la fin ; pas de it répété.
  2. The bus isn't big enough for all the students to fit in.enough après l'adjectif.
  3. It's important for you to revise every evening.It's + adjectif + for + qqn + to.
  4. I left the key under the mat for my brother to get in.« pour que » avec un autre sujet → for + qqn + to.

4Traduction · 1 pt par phrase

  1. Léa had her computer repaired by a friend.Aussi juste : got her computer repaired. Participe passé obligatoire.
  2. She asked him to check her phone too.ask + qqn + to. « lui » = l'ami → him.
  3. The computer is now much faster than before.« bien plus » = much / far / a lot, jamais very.
  4. The novel was too long for her to read in one evening.Pas de it après read.
  5. Her parents let her go out on Saturday evening.let + her + verbe sans to. allowed her to go out est aussi juste.
  6. The concert made her so happy that she called all her friends.make + qqn + adjectif = rendre.
